# Third-party notices / Avisos de terceros

## `mtkcam-raw`

`vendor/mtkcam-raw/` contains code from
[ReCoreShift/mtkcam-raw](https://github.com/ReCoreShift/mtkcam-raw), used for
MediaTek AArch64 ELF analysis. It is licensed under MPL-2.0; the full text is
in [`vendor/mtkcam-raw/LICENSE`](vendor/mtkcam-raw/LICENSE). Local changes must
retain the notices required by that license.

## MediaTek / Transsion firmware libraries

`stock-binaries/` and the complete RAW modules contain camera libraries from
MediaTek/Transsion firmware. They remain third-party proprietary binaries. The
project records their hashes for reproducibility but does not grant a license
or redistribution rights. Obtain the necessary rights before publishing or
redistributing these files.

## GCam payload

`gcam/apatch-startup/payload/` contains a version-locked, modified APK based on
the BSG Google Camera port and a modified native library. Those files include
third-party Google/BSG material. They are not covered by the MPL-2.0 notice or
by a project-wide license. Confirm the applicable distribution terms before
publishing them.
