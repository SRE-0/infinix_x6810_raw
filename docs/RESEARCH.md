# Historical research index

The active entry points are [development](DEVELOPMENT.md), [compatibility](COMPATIBILITY.md) and [flash analysis](FLASH_COMPATIBILITY.md). Original experiments are preserved under `archive/legacy-project/`; references to a “current” state in old reports apply to their date.

| Material | Location |
|---|---|

| Vendor/framework patch deltas | `archive/legacy-project/patches/` |
| Integrated flash experiments | `archive/legacy-project/modules/` |
| Original reports and tools | `archive/legacy-project/docs/`, `archive/legacy-project/tools/` |
| Original migration inventory | [inventory.json](inventory.json) |

Historical scripts can require local firmware, dependencies and device state. They are not the release build interface. GCam tools and reports live in [the independent GCam repository](https://github.com/SRE-0/infinix_x6810_gcam_port). Historical reports retain their original language; maintained guidance defaults to English.
